Japan Visa Requirements for Pakistani Citizens

Japan is a popular destination for travelers interested in its culture, technology, history, and beautiful places. Before planning a trip, Pakistani citizens should understand the visa requirements and application process.

Types of Japan Visas

Japan offers different visa categories depending on the purpose of travel. These may include tourist, business, student, and family visit visas.

Tourist
Sightseeing and leisure travel.
Business
Meetings, conferences, and other work-related trips.
Student
Study at a school or educational institution.
Family visit
Visiting relatives or family members in Japan.

Required Documents

Gather these before you start your application.

  • Valid Pakistani passport
  • Completed visa application form
  • Recent passport-size photograph
  • CNIC copy
  • Financial supporting documents
  • Travel itinerary
  • Additional documents according to visa category

How to Apply for a Japan Visa
